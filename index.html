Normal move = how far price typically moves for this much net delta, learned from the last hour of 1‑minute bars on this page. Absorbed = share of that expected move that didn't happen. High absorption means passive limit orders are soaking up aggressive flow: the side doing the absorbing usually wins once the aggressor runs out, unless price breaks through the absorbing level.
